How it works

  1. Get an invite. You receive the Deal Radar extension for Chrome or Firefox directly — it isn't in any public store.
  2. Log in with Telegram from the extension (popup → Watches). No password, no e-mail. Alerts arrive in the same Telegram account.
  3. Create a watch. Keywords that must appear, keywords that must not, a minimum and maximum price in your currency — CZK, EUR, PLN or USD (listings in other currencies are converted at the daily ČNB rate), the sources and, for Facebook, the groups to search. A 7-day preview shows what it would have caught.
  4. Get the alert. Every source is polled once a minute. A new listing that matches lands in Telegram with title, price (also in your currency) and link — you message the seller.
  5. Vote Good or Junk. One tap per alert tells the radar how well a watch is tuned.

Facebook Marketplace and groups, in your own browser

Browser extension

FB has no public search API, so the extension checks your searches from your normal, logged-in Chrome or Firefox — one pinned tab, every ~3 minutes, newest first, Prague + 500 km.

Your FB groups

Many deals are posted only in collector groups. On the FB groups tab you tick up to 10 groups you've joined; each watch then picks which of them to search. The extension runs Facebook's own search inside each group for the watch's keywords, newest posts first, about every 10 minutes.

Only offers, only for you

A group post alerts only if it looks like an offer — a price or a sale word (prodám, na prodej, cenu nabídněte, sprzedam…) and no “wanted” word (koupím, hledám, kupię…). Group posts are private: they alert only the person whose browser found them, never go into the shared index and aren't part of the bidding.

Nothing leaves but listings

The extension pushes only the cards and posts it sees (title, price, link) to the radar. Your Facebook session stays in your browser.

Other sources need no extension

Bazoš, Aukro, Sbazar, Vinted, OLX.pl, Allegro.cz, willhaben.at, Kleinanzeigen.de and eBay.de/.at/.com are polled by the server itself, so alerts keep coming when your computer is off.

Bid to hear first

When one listing matches several people's watches, the highest bid is alerted first; each next place a minute later. Alerts show your rank and the top rival bid.
